# Rendering issues concerning translucent objects in the world

- English

In the game, since the rendering order has been **completely hardcoded** by Mojang, but the **depth range** of translucent objects during rendering is not dynamically calculated, and rendering is performed immediately, this leads to the following issue: if an object (except clouds) satisfies both conditions of **being rendered later** and **being far away**, the parts occluded by nearby translucent objects will be **discarded and not rendered**, and will not participate in translucent blending.

To fix this, one would almost need to rewrite the entire **rendering logic**, but we currently do not have the capability to do so...

Known phenomena include:
- Nearby water occluding distant water
- Nearby water occluding the translucent parts of distant slime blocks
- When looking down from above, clouds occlude the translucent parts of water and slime blocks(**Fixed**)

- 中文

在游戏中，由于渲染顺序已经被Mojang**彻底写死**，但半透明事物在渲染时的**深度范围**并不是动态计算的，同时，渲染是立即进行的，这就导致，如果一个事物(除了云)同时满足**后渲染，在远处**这几个条件，其被近处半透明事物所遮挡住的部分便会被**丢弃不渲染**，不参与到半透明混合中。

如果要解决，几乎需要重新写一遍**渲染逻辑**，但目前我们没有这个能力去进行……

目前已知的现象有：
- 近处水遮挡远处水
- 近处水遮挡远处史莱姆块的半透明部分
- 从空中向下俯视时云层遮挡水和史莱姆块的半透明部分(**已解决**)
